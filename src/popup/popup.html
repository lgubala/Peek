Hover delay
Peek makes network requests. When "read pages on hover" is on, resting on a link asks that site for its page. The site sees a request from your IP address and can log it.
  • No cookies are sent or stored, and no referrer is passed.
  • The site's JavaScript never runs, so its trackers do not fire.
  • Nothing is added to your browsing history.
  • Links that log out, unsubscribe, confirm, pay or carry one-time tokens are never fetched.
