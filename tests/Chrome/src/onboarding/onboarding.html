Peek is installed

One thing to know, and one thing to try.

Hold Alt and hover a link

Nothing happens until you do. Hold the key, rest the pointer on any link, and a card shows what is actually on the other side — a recipe's ingredients and steps, an article's text, a product's price, a repository's README.

Try it on this one: a peephole

Peek fetches pages

To show you a page, Peek has to ask for it. The site sees a request from your IP address and can log it, exactly as it would a visit. That only ever happens for a link you deliberately held the key on.

  • No cookies are sent or stored, and no referrer is passed.
  • The site's JavaScript never runs, so its trackers do not fire.
  • Nothing is added to your browsing history.
  • Links that log out, unsubscribe, confirm or pay are never fetched.
  • Move the pointer on and Peek stops asking.

If you would rather it never fetched anything, the toolbar button has a switch for that — Peek then tells you what it can read from the link itself, which is more than you might expect.
